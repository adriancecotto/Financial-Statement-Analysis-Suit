Reference Manual for System Financial Formulas & Metrics

1. Income Statement Formulas

  • Gross Margin: Sales Revenues − Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
  • EBITDA: Gross Margin − Selling & Administrative Expenses (and other operating items)
  • EBIT (Operating Income): EBITDA − Depreciation & Amortization
  • EBT (Pre-tax Income): EBIT − Interest Expenses
  • Net Income: EBT − Taxes
  • Addition to Retained Earnings: Net Income − Dividends Paid

2. Balance Sheet Formulas

  • Total Current Assets: Cash & Cash Equivalents + Accounts Receivable + Inventory
  • Total Non-Current Assets: Property, Plant & Equipment (Net) + Intangible Assets
  • TOTAL ASSETS: Total Current Assets + Total Non-Current Assets
  • Total Current Liabilities: Accounts Payable + Notes Payable / Short-Term Debt
  • Total Non-Current Liabilities: Long-Term Debt
  • TOTAL LIABILITIES: Total Current Liabilities + Total Non-Current Liabilities
  • TOTAL EQUITY: Share Capital / Common Stock + Retained Earnings
  • TOTAL LIABILITIES & EQUITY: Total Liabilities + Total Equity
  • Balance Sheet Identity: Total Assets ≡ Total Liabilities + Total Equity

3. Cash Flow Statement Formulas

  • Net Cash from Operating Activities: Net Income + Depreciation & Amortization ± Changes in Working Capital
  • Net Cash from Investing Activities: Capital Expenditures (CapEx) ± Other Investments
  • Net Cash from Financing Activities: New Debt Issued / Repaid − Dividends Paid
  • Net Change in Cash: Operating Cash Flow + Investing Cash Flow + Financing Cash Flow

4. Liquidity Ratios

  • Current Ratio: Total Current Assets ÷ Total Current Liabilities
  • Quick Ratio (Acid-Test): (Total Current Assets − Inventory) ÷ Total Current Liabilities
  • Cash Ratio: Cash & Cash Equivalents ÷ Total Current Liabilities

5. Asset Turnover / Efficiency Ratios

  • Total Asset Turnover: Sales Revenues ÷ Total Assets
  • Inventory Turnover: Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) ÷ Inventory
  • Receivables Turnover: Sales Revenues ÷ Accounts Receivable

6. Financial Leverage & Coverage Ratios

  • Total Debt Ratio: Total Liabilities ÷ Total Assets
  • Debt-to-Equity (D/E) Ratio: Total Liabilities ÷ Total Equity
  • Equity Multiplier: Total Assets ÷ Total Equity
  • Times Interest Earned (TIE): EBIT ÷ Interest Expenses
  • Cash Coverage Ratio: EBITDA ÷ Interest Expenses

7. Profitability & Growth Ratios

  • Profit Margin: Net Income ÷ Sales Revenues
  • Return on Assets (ROA): Net Income ÷ Total Assets
  • Return on Equity (ROE): Net Income ÷ Total Equity
  • Retention Ratio (b): Addition to Retained Earnings ÷ Net Income
  • Internal Growth Rate (IGR): (ROA × b) ÷ (1 − [ROA × b])
  • Sustainable Growth Rate (SGR): (ROE × b) ÷ (1 − [ROE × b])

8. Corporate Finance Cash Flows (Free Cash Flow Identity)

  • Operating Cash Flow (OCF): EBIT + Depreciation & Amortization − Taxes
  • Capital Spending (Net CapEx): Ending Net Fixed Assets − Beginning Net Fixed Assets + Depreciation
  • Change in Net Working Capital (NWC): Ending NWC − Beginning NWC
  • Cash Flow From Assets (CFFA): OCF − Net CapEx − Change in NWC
  • Cash Flow to Creditors: Interest Expenses − Net New Long-Term Borrowing
  • Cash Flow to Stockholders: Dividends Paid − Net New Equity Raised
  • Cash Flow Identity Verification: Cash Flow From Assets ≡ Cash Flow to Creditors + Cash Flow to Stockholders
